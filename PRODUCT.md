# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS with minimal JS, deployed on Netlify (user's choice). Replaces the 2018 webpack 4 + node-sass + jQuery + Bootstrap build. Contact form and Netlify `sendmail` function are dropped.

## Users

Primary: business owners and SMEs (Thailand and the region) who need a custom web, IoT or AI product built or rescued, deciding whether Weerayut can be trusted to ship and run it. Secondary: companies hiring a software or engineering lead.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Weerayut Teja (Software Development Manager at an IoT solutions company in Chiang Mai; 15+ years). Success: a visitor understands in seconds what he builds and for whom, believes it from real shipped work, and emails him. Hiring enquiries are welcome but secondary.

## Positioning

A senior engineer who both leads teams and still builds and operates the whole stack (development, DevOps, SRE), from IoT monitoring to AI-powered SaaS, for Thai and regional businesses.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, or a referral and judge quickly on a laptop or phone. Contact is by email only; LINE is not yet public.

- Languages and stacks stated by the user and README: C#/.NET, Node.js/TypeScript, Go, Python, Dart/Flutter, React, Next.js, NestJS, Vue, Angular; PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Docker, AWS, GCP.

Old site also linked CodePen, Twitter, Facebook, Instagram; user said everything "already contained in older website", read as: carry over those links unless dropped later (open decision, default: GitHub, LinkedIn, Medium only in primary placement).

## Product Principles

1. Name only real work, and say plainly whose it is (own build, team build, freelance).
2. One clear way to reach him: email.
3. Show how he thinks and operates, not a skills inventory.
4. Fast and light: static, few requests, no heavy animation.
5. Senior and calm, never template-like.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Real alt text. English only.
